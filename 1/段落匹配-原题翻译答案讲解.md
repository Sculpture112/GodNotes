## UNIT 1 - An impressive English lesson

#### 一、原题

**41.** The writer met one of his student who had just finished the European trip. Then he restarted to seriously think about the way he taught his son about English grammar.

**答案出处说明：** 原文出处与题目表达的是同一件事或同一层逻辑关系。

### 第 41 题

**41.** The rule of the game is that every time students click to open a door, they will use one of the 100 clicks, but they will not get any money.

**对应段落 F：** In the experiment sessions, students played a computer game that provided cash behind three doors appearing on the screen.
