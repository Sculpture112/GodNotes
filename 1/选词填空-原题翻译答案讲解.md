# 选词填空：原题、翻译与答案讲解

## UNIT 1

### 一、原题

The Internet has made learning English much easier. English learners used to be **C. obliged** to spend their time in libraries looking for the books that would help them in their language learning. It was very inconvenient because a lot of materials were **L. hidden** in numerous textbooks and readers. But today content on a variety of subjects is only a click away. This is especially **E. beneficial** for those who are serious about learning English.

In order to achieve **A. fluency** in English, you need to be comfortable using at least 10,000 words. The **B. abundant** materials on the Internet make it possible for you to choose appropriate content to read and listen to. These materials can be **D. adjusted** to your level if you input some keywords in the search engine. But how can you remember the **F. bulk** of unfamiliar words?

In this case, the Internet **I. evidently** makes it easier for you to learn vocabulary. You can use online dictionaries to instantly find out the specific meanings and grammatical **K. functions** of new words. The Internet helps you **H. efficiently** build up your vocabulary based on lively and interesting language content, which greatly reduces the **G. distress** caused by your inability to remember the new words. The efficiency of this vocabulary learning is one of the reasons why the Internet has become a/an **J. ideal** place to learn the English language.

### 二、翻译

互联网让英语学习变得容易得多。过去，英语学习者不得不把时间花在图书馆里，寻找那些能够帮助他们学习语言的书籍。这非常不方便，因为大量资料都隐藏在众多教材和读物之中。但如今，各种主题的内容只需点击一下就能获得。这对那些认真学习英语的人尤其有益。

为了达到英语流利的程度，你需要能够熟练使用至少一万个单词。互联网上丰富的资料使你能够选择合适的内容来阅读和收听。如果你在搜索引擎中输入一些关键词，这些资料还可以调整到适合你的水平。但是，你怎样才能记住大量不熟悉的单词呢？

在这种情况下，互联网显然让词汇学习变得更容易。你可以使用在线词典立即查到新单词的具体含义和语法功能。互联网帮助你基于生动有趣的语言内容高效地积累词汇，这大大减少了你因无法记住新单词而产生的苦恼。这种词汇学习的高效性，是互联网成为学习英语的理想场所的原因之一。

### 三、答案解释讲解

- **C. obliged**：be obliged to do sth. 表示“不得不做某事”，符合过去学习者必须去图书馆找资料的语境。
- **L. hidden**：资料“隐藏”在大量教材和读物中，说明过去查找资料不方便。
- **E. beneficial**：be beneficial for/to 表示“对……有益”，这里指互联网内容对认真学英语的人有帮助。
- **A. fluency**：achieve fluency in English 表示“达到英语流利程度”，搭配自然。
- **B. abundant**：abundant materials 表示“丰富的资料”，说明互联网上资源很多。
- **D. adjusted**：be adjusted to your level 表示“被调整到适合你的水平”，符合个性化学习语境。
- **F. bulk**：the bulk of unfamiliar words 表示“大量不熟悉的单词”。
- **I. evidently**：副词修饰 makes，evidently 表示“显然”，用于总结互联网的作用。
- **K. functions**：grammatical functions 表示“语法功能”，是固定搭配。
- **H. efficiently**：副词修饰 build up your vocabulary，表示“高效地积累词汇”。
- **G. distress**：distress 表示“苦恼、痛苦”，对应无法记住新词带来的困扰。
- **J. ideal**：an ideal place 表示“理想的地方”，总结互联网适合学习英语。

## UNIT 2

### 一、原题

When choosing a college or major, you need to examine yourself: What are your **A. abilities** and strengths? What are your **L. weaknesses**? What do you want out of life? Are you socially **B. competent** or do you need family and friends' support in order to **C. confirm** what you have decided is right?

Your college does not have to be bigger than your high school. Don't be **F. obsessed** with the size of your college. Since a smaller community is more **K. favorable** for self-exploration, many **I. reputable** colleges have a smaller student population. It is not the number of people, but the people themselves and the kind of school community in which you will learn that really matter.

You don't have to **J. resort** to other people's help, but think about the people in your life who are happy and successful and find out where they went to college. You will **E. inevitably** find that success in life has less to do with the choice of college than with the experience and opportunities you'll **D. gain** while in college, coupled with personal qualities and traits.

There are many **G. options** to major in, but you may need the variety and depth of college coursework to determine the major you are **H. passionate** about. Whether you choose your major out of personal interest or for the needs of society and the country, you may possibly change your mind, even two or three times, before you finally settle on one.

### 二、翻译

在选择大学或专业时，你需要审视自己：你的能力和优势是什么？你的弱点是什么？你想从生活中得到什么？你的社交能力是否足够，还是需要家人和朋友的支持来确认你所做的决定是正确的？

你的大学不一定要比你的高中大。不要过分迷恋大学的规模。由于较小的校园群体更有利于自我探索，许多声誉良好的大学学生人数反而较少。真正重要的不是人数多少，而是那些与你一起学习的人，以及你将在其中学习的学校共同体。

你不必求助于别人，而可以想想你生活中那些快乐且成功的人，了解他们曾经上过哪所大学。你会不可避免地发现，人生成功与大学选择的关系并没有那么大，反而更多取决于你在大学期间获得的经历和机会，以及个人品质和性格特征。

可选择的专业有很多，但你可能需要通过大学课程的广度和深度来确定自己真正热爱的专业。无论你是出于个人兴趣选择专业，还是为了社会和国家的需要而选择专业，在最终确定之前，你都有可能改变想法，甚至改变两三次。

### 三、答案解释讲解

- **A. abilities**：abilities and strengths 表示“能力和优势”，与后面的 weaknesses 形成对照。
- **L. weaknesses**：weaknesses 表示“弱点、劣势”，用于自我审视。
- **B. competent**：socially competent 表示“有社交能力的、能胜任社交的”。
- **C. confirm**：confirm what you have decided is right 表示“确认自己的决定是正确的”。
- **F. obsessed**：be obsessed with 表示“痴迷于、过分关注”，这里指不要只盯着大学规模。
- **K. favorable**：be favorable for 表示“有利于”，较小的环境更有利于自我探索。
- **I. reputable**：reputable colleges 表示“声誉良好的大学”。
- **J. resort**：resort to other people's help 表示“求助于别人”，是固定搭配。
- **E. inevitably**：inevitably 表示“不可避免地”，强调经过观察后自然会发现这个结论。
- **D. gain**：gain experience and opportunities 表示“获得经历和机会”。
- **G. options**：options to major in 表示“可选择的专业选项”。
- **H. passionate**：be passionate about 表示“对……充满热情”，用于说明真正感兴趣的专业。

If companies want to keep on these talented employees, they have to make the workplace more **C. flexible**. Many companies have taken significant steps to create more **D. dynamic** working environments, in an attempt to encourage employees to stay longer with the company.

王女士对她在一家软件公司的新工作很满意。她说：“这份工作为我的发展提供了一个很大的平台，而且我能学到很多东西！”和王女士一样，越来越多的中国年轻劳动者要求自己的声音被听见，并且在职场中更加主动地表达自我。根据今年早些时候发布的一份报告，年轻人已经成为中国人才市场的主体。因此，许多公司正在加倍努力吸引并留住这一重要群体。

这一代人成长于中国社会和经济发生巨大变化的时期，已经成为受过良好教育、资源整合能力很强的职场成员。他们常常寻求更好的学习机会和职业发展机会。一项关于这一代人的研究发现，在同一家公司工作一到五年的员工中，超过 80% 的人表示，良好的职业发展是促使他们留在公司的原因。

这一代人并不只是想服从命令。他们想参与决策。他们质疑权威，并且会坚持自己追求的目标。如果公司想留住这些有才华的员工，就必须让工作场所更加灵活。许多公司已经采取了重要措施，创造更加充满活力的工作环境，以鼓励员工在公司待得更久。

### 三、答案解释讲解

- **L. platform**：platform for me to develop 表示“供我发展的平台”，符合工作机会带来成长空间的语境。
- **B. asserting**：assert oneself 表示“坚持自己的主张、表现自我”，这里指年轻人在职场中更敢发声。
- **J. released**：a report released earlier this year 表示“今年早些时候发布的报告”。
- **F. retain**：attract and retain 表示“吸引并留住”，常用于人才管理语境。
- **E. emerged**：emerge as 表示“作为……出现、成为”，说明这一代人成为职场重要力量。
- **G. remained**：remain with the same company 表示“留在同一家公司”。
- **H. motivated**：motivate sb. to do sth. 表示“促使某人做某事”，这里指职业发展促使员工留下。
- **I. obey**：obey orders 表示“服从命令”，与后文参与决策形成对比。
- **A. participate**：participate in decision-making 表示“参与决策”，搭配准确。
- **K. persevering**：persevering 表示“坚持不懈的”，说明年轻人会坚持自己的追求。
- **C. flexible**：flexible workplace 表示“灵活的工作场所”，符合留住年轻人才的措施。
- **D. dynamic**：dynamic working environments 表示“充满活力的工作环境”。

## UNIT 5

### 一、原题

To spend or save is a question which many people have in their mind. They are always in a(n) **B. dilemma** whether they should spend the money that they have earned or save that money for the future. Well, there is no **D. explicit** answer to the question as different people have different **I. perspectives** on life, and that is the reason why some people **C. tend** to spend all their money while others **J. retain** control over their money.

People who spend all their money do not think much about the future. What they enjoy doing is to get pleasure from spending money. For example, if they like a(n) **H. particular** car, mobile phone, or laptop, they will buy it without giving it a second thought. For them, the most important thing is to satisfy their growing material **A. appetites**.

In the long run, saving helps people **F. manage** their personal finances as saving helps an individual better plan for future **L. urgent** needs. From our everyday life, we can see that individuals who can **K. suspend** their spending save money successfully. Instead of buying things on impulse, they delay their purchasing decision and are not easily **G. manipulated** by commercial ads. Individuals who have **E. responsibility** for their family or their loved ones often belong to this group.

### 二、翻译

花钱还是存钱，是许多人心中都会有的问题。他们总是陷入一种两难境地：究竟应该把自己赚来的钱花掉，还是为将来把钱存起来。其实，这个问题没有明确的答案，因为不同的人对生活有不同的看法，这就是为什么有些人倾向于花光所有的钱，而另一些人则会控制自己的钱。

花光所有钱的人通常不太考虑未来。他们喜欢做的事就是从花钱中获得快乐。例如，如果他们喜欢某一辆车、某一部手机或某一台笔记本电脑，他们会不假思索地买下来。对他们来说，最重要的是满足自己不断增长的物质欲望。

从长远来看，储蓄能够帮助人们管理个人财务，因为储蓄能帮助个人更好地为未来的紧急需求作计划。在日常生活中，我们可以看到，那些能够暂停消费的人往往能成功存钱。他们不会冲动购物，而是推迟购买决定，也不容易被商业广告操控。那些对家庭或所爱之人负有责任的人通常属于这一类。

### 三、答案解释讲解

- **B. dilemma**：in a dilemma 表示“处于两难境地”，对应花钱还是存钱的选择困难。
- **D. explicit**：explicit answer 表示“明确的答案”，说明这个问题没有统一答案。
- **I. perspectives**：perspectives on life 表示“人生观、对生活的看法”。
- **C. tend**：tend to do sth. 表示“倾向于做某事”，说明有些人倾向于花光钱。
- **J. retain**：retain control over money 表示“保持对金钱的控制”。
- **H. particular**：a particular car 表示“某一辆特定的车”，用于举例说明具体想买的东西。
- **A. appetites**：material appetites 表示“物质欲望”，符合不断消费的语境。
- **F. manage**：manage personal finances 表示“管理个人财务”。
- **L. urgent**：urgent needs 表示“紧急需求”，说明储蓄可以应对未来急需。
- **K. suspend**：suspend spending 表示“暂停消费、暂缓花钱”。
- **G. manipulated**：be manipulated by commercial ads 表示“被商业广告操控”。
- **E. responsibility**：have responsibility for 表示“对……负有责任”，说明有家庭责任的人更愿意存钱。

## UNIT 6

### 一、原题

Being simple is not necessarily about having less. It can be about having more: more time, more enjoyment, more accomplishment, and more of what **H. motivates** you.

If some activities don't bring you any benefits, doing less or none of them makes sense, because it may eventually do harm to you to **C. discard** all of them. The **F. purpose** of being simple is to remove what's not important.

To understand what should be **C. discarded**, try to think of activities as either assets or "obligations". An asset is something that is valuable. One **I. sensible** example is learning to manage your money. A little more broadly, an asset is anything that can strengthen and **K. preserve** you, moving you closer to your goals. However, the "obligations" are a kind of **J. passive** debt, anything that weakens you, moves you farther from your goals, provides negative stress, and creates anxiety.

Then how can you **E. implement** the idea of being simple? Think about your daily activities and set a(n) **A. agenda**. You list your daily **B. routines** such as paying bills and planning a birthday party for a friend. Don't just **L. revolve** around your hobbies. Ask yourself if the activities get you closer to your goals. If not, **G. modify** the list: remove what is unnecessary and concentrate on what is more important for you.

### 二、翻译

简单生活并不一定意味着拥有更少。它也可以意味着拥有更多：更多时间、更多乐趣、更多成就，以及更多真正激励你的东西。

如果有些活动不能给你带来任何好处，那么少做甚至不做这些活动是有道理的，因为把它们全部抛弃最终也可能对你造成伤害。简单生活的目的，是去除那些不重要的东西。

要理解哪些东西应该被舍弃，可以试着把活动看作“资产”或“负担”。资产是有价值的东西。一个明智的例子就是学会管理你的金钱。更广泛地说，资产是任何能够增强并维护你、让你更接近目标的东西。然而，“负担”是一种被动的债务，指任何削弱你、让你远离目标、带来负面压力并制造焦虑的东西。

那么，怎样才能实践简单生活的理念呢？想想你的日常活动，并制定一个日程安排。你列出日常事务，比如支付账单、为朋友策划生日聚会。不要只围绕你的爱好打转。问问自己这些活动是否让你更接近目标。如果不是，就修改清单：去掉不必要的事情，把注意力集中在对你更重要的事情上。

### 三、答案解释讲解

- **H. motivates**：what motivates you 表示“激励你的东西”，说明简单生活可以让人拥有更多真正有动力的事物。
- **C. discard**：discard all of them 表示“把它们全部抛弃”，discard 意为“丢弃、舍弃”。
- **F. purpose**：the purpose of being simple 表示“简单生活的目的”，引出核心观点。
- **C. discarded**：what should be discarded 表示“哪些东西应该被舍弃”，与 remove what's not important 呼应。
- **I. sensible**：sensible example 表示“合理的、明智的例子”。
- **K. preserve**：strengthen and preserve you 表示“增强并维护你”，与资产的积极作用一致。
- **J. passive**：passive debt 表示“被动债务”，这里比喻那些消耗你、拖累你的活动。
- **E. implement**：implement the idea 表示“实施、实践这个理念”。
